Exploring Diabetes Risk Factors

Diabetes is a growing public health challenge in the United States, affecting millions of adults each year. As Type 2 diabetes is largely preventable, identifying risk factors such as physical activity, smoking, and blood pressure levels etc can help guide preventive interventions and health education efforts.

Project Goals

  • Identify behavioral factors (physical activity, diet, smoking) that are associated with diabetes prevalence.
  • Examine demographic characteristics (age, gender, income, education) related to diabetes risk.
  • Develop a predictive model to estimate individual diabetes risk based on health behaviors and demographics.
  • Evaluate the performance of the predictive model using appropriate metrics
